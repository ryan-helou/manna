# Manna

**A Bible reading plan app for a small group of friends that takes you through the whole Bible, paced by how long each chapter takes to read.**

</div>

<br>

Most reading plans give you the same number of chapters each day, even though some chapters are much longer or harder than others. Manna estimates how many minutes each of the 1,189 chapters takes to read and understand, then spreads them out so each day takes about the same amount of time. Some days are one chapter, others are five. It is made for friends reading together, and can be added to a phone's home screen like a regular app.

## What you can do with it

- See today's chapters, open each one to read it, and tick them off as you go.
- Choose how long the plan should take, from 1 to 13 months, and invite friends with a short four-character code.
- See whether you're ahead or behind, and exactly which days you missed.
- Track your streak, see a calendar-style grid of your progress, and earn milestones as you finish sections of the Bible.
- See how the rest of the group is doing, and who has read today.
- Leave notes on a chapter, reply to friends and react with emoji.
- Answer three quick multiple-choice questions on what you just read, see a verse of the day, and get an optional evening reminder if you haven't read yet.
